How to read this. Prevalence is the share of respondents in a group who report diabetes (BRFSS code 2); prediabetes is shown separately. Figures are self-reported survey answers, so they show associations, not causes, and are not clinical diagnoses. Age bands follow the BRFSS 5-year age codes (1 = 18-24 … 13 = 80+) grouped into six bands. Groups with fewer than 100 respondents are flagged in the tooltip.

Built as the population-level companion to ChroniCare, the chronic-condition tracking app: the risk factors explored here (blood pressure, cholesterol, BMI, activity, general health) are the same kinds of readings a patient tracks day to day.
